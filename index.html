04 / How I Build

Six phases. No hand-waving. Every project, every time.

The same governance model I use inside enterprise banks. Adapted to the size of the engagement and the maturity of your team.

01

Discovery & constraint mapping

Two-week sprint: failure modes, stakeholder map, compliance constraints, and a real-cost view of the manual process. The plan is grounded in what your audit team will actually accept.

02

Architecture & guardrail design

Pre-LLM guardrails, model selection, post-LLM validation, tool calls, HITL queues, verification. Designed with your security team in the room, not presented to them afterwards.

03

Prototype with real data

No fake demos on cherry-picked records. Working prototype against a representative slice of your actual document corpus, with measured accuracy and failure-mode analysis.

04

Evaluation framework first

Golden dataset, regression suite, and observability wired in before scaling. You ship a system that knows when it's failing, not one that needs a customer to tell you.

05

Phased production rollout

Start at 70% automation with HITL on the rest. Use the human-review queue as training signal. Reduce review percentage as confidence proves out. No big-bang launches.

06

Handover & team enablement

Runbooks, prompt versioning practices, eval cadence, and a sit-along with your engineers so they can extend the system. I work to make myself unnecessary, deliberately.
